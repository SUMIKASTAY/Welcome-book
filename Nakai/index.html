Appliance instructions

Television (TV) Instructions

  1. Turn ON / OFF
    Press the red power button at the top of the remote.
  2. Change channels
    Use the ▲ / ▼ buttons next to “チャンネル”.
  3. Adjust volume
    Use the ＋ / － buttons next to “音量”.
  4. Direction buttons
    Use the large center arrows (← ↑ → ↓) to navigate menus if needed.

📺 You may log in to streaming services with your own account if available.
Please remember to log out before check-out.

💡 If the TV does not turn on, please make sure the power cable is connected and press the power button on the TV itself.

Washing Machine (Auto Detergent)

  1. Open the door and place your laundry inside.
    *Max capacity: 10 kg*
  2. Press & hold the power button (①) to turn on.
  3. Select mode using the mode button (②):
    • 洗い — Wash only
    • すすぎ＋脱水 — Rinse & spin
    • 脱水＋乾燥 — Spin & dry
    • 洗濯＋乾燥 — Full wash & dry (recommended)
  4. Once you have selected the mode, press START / PAUSE (③) to begin.

🧴 No need to add detergent.
The washing machine will automatically dispense detergent.

💡 *Drying may take longer depending on load size.* ⚠ *Please remove lint filter content if full, and do not overload the drum.*

Air Conditioner (AC)

  1. Power ON / OFF
    Press the 運転 / 停止 or 停止 button (often yellow or red).
  2. Select mode
    Choose one of the main buttons:
    • 冷房 – Cool (for summer / cooling)
    • 暖房 – Warm (for winter / heating)
    • 除湿 / ドライ – Dehumidify / Dry (removes moisture, mild cooling)
    • 自動 – Auto (AC chooses the best mode automatically)
  3. Set the temperature
    Use the 温度 ▲ / ▼ buttons to adjust the temperature.
    Recommended: 24–27 °C in summer, 20–23 °C in winter.
  4. Adjust fan speed
    Press 風量 (Fan speed) to change the airflow strength.
  5. Adjust wind direction
    Use 風向 上下 / 左右 buttons to move the air up/down or left/right.

💡 Please close windows & doors while using the AC for better efficiency.
⚠ Always turn OFF the AC when you leave the room or the house.

Microwave Oven

  1. Open the door and place your food or drink inside on the plate.
  2. Choose the mode:
    • 500W — Standard heating (recommended)
    • 300W — Use for frozen food
    Press the mode button (highlighted in the picture) to switch modes.
  3. Rotate the timer dial to set the heating time.
    *E.g. 1–2 min for warming drinks, 3–5 min for meals.
  4. The microwave will start automatically** after turning the dial.
  5. When the time is up, it will stop and make a sound. Carefully remove the food.

⚠ Please do not use metal containers, aluminum foil, or sealed containers inside the microwave. Use microwave-safe dishes only.

Water Heater Panel

⚠️ Do NOT turn OFF the water heater switch — it must remain ON to supply hot water and heating.
  1. Power Switch (運転)
    This button turns the water heater ON / OFF.
    ⚠ Please do not turn OFF the water heater during your stay. If it is off, there will be no hot water.
  2. Temperature Adjustment (給湯 温度調節)
    Use the ▲ / ▼ buttons to change the hot-water temperature.
    Recommended setting: 40–43 °C for showers and baths.

💡 If the water feels too hot or too cold, please adjust here rather than at the shower tap.

Smart Toilet

  1. Stop (关闭 / STOP)
    Press the 止 button to stop all functions.
  2. Washing the buttocks (清洗臀部)
    Press the おしり button.
    *The nozzle will spray warm water for cleaning.*
  3. Bidet / Gentle cleaning (女性用 / ビデ)
    Press the ビデ button.
  4. Adjust cleaning intensity (清洗强度)
    Use the ＋ / − buttons to make the spray stronger or softer.
  5. Adjust water temperature (温度调节)
    Press the 温水温度 button to change the water temperature.
  6. Adjust seat temperature (座圈温度)
    Press the 便座温度 button to warm or cool the toilet seat.

💡 The toilet will automatically stop after a short time. ⚠ Please do not stand on the toilet seat.

Laundry Drying Instructions

You can dry your laundry in the following places:

  • 2nd-floor balcony — ideal for drying clothes in the open air.
  • Bathroom (indoor drying) — hang your clothes inside the bathroom (recommended).

For indoor drying:

  1. Find the ventilation/heater control panel in the bathroom.
  2. Press the left yellow button to start Dry mode (乾燥).
  3. Optionally set a timer; it will stop automatically.

Feel free to use these spaces as needed.

House Rules

Garbage disposal rules

We understand that garbage rules in Japan can be confusing, so we made it simple. Separate the garbage into 2 categories using different plastic bags, then put them in the silver dust box in the parking lot as below.

Burnable Garbage
  • Kitchen waste
  • Paper scraps
  • Clothing, etc.
Recyclable Garbage
  • Plastic bottles
  • Container jars & cans
  • Aerosol spray cans, etc.

Garbage disposal II

Except for the rules above, please do not leave used luggage or any big items in the house. If you leave items that are not permitted, we may ask you to pay for the costs incurred.

Quiet time: 10pm–8am

Please respect our residential community. Between 9pm and 8am, keep noise to a minimum.

Please take off your shoes

Please do not wear your shoes inside the property.

Smoking rules

This is a non‑smoking home — no smoking or vaping inside the house.

A designated smoking area is available on the terrace on the 4th floor. Do not smoke on the street — it is strictly prohibited in Tokyo.

Parking is not available on-site.

If you are arriving by car, please use one of the nearby coin-operated parking lots.

security

For security reasons, a surveillance camera is installed at the entrance, as required by local government guidance.

Pets

We love animals, but this home is pet‑free due to allergies.

Additional guests

Please do not bring additional guests. Exceeding the maximum occupancy is not permitted.

Breakages

If you break anything, please tell us immediately. Minor issues are usually not charged. For larger issues (e.g., TV screen), we will agree the cost with you before checkout to avoid surprises.

Written exceptions

Any exceptions to these rules must be approved in advance. Thank you!
