Washing Machine (Auto Detergent)

  1. Open the door and place your laundry inside.
    *Max capacity: 10 kg*
  2. Press & hold the power button (①) to turn on.
  3. Select mode using the mode button (②):
    • 洗い — Wash only
    • すすぎ＋脱水 — Rinse & spin
    • 脱水＋乾燥 — Spin & dry
    • 洗濯＋乾燥 — Full wash & dry (recommended)
  4. Once you have selected the mode, press START / PAUSE (③) to begin.

🧴 No need to add detergent.
The washing machine will automatically dispense detergent.

💡 *Drying may take longer depending on load size.* ⚠ *Please remove lint filter content if full, and do not overload the drum.*

Air Conditioner (AC)

  1. Power ON / OFF
    Press the 運転 / 停止 or 停止 button (often yellow or red).
  2. Select mode
    Choose one of the main buttons:
    • 冷房 – Cool (for summer / cooling)
    • 暖房 – Warm (for winter / heating)
    • 除湿 / ドライ – Dehumidify / Dry (removes moisture, mild cooling)
    • 自動 – Auto (AC chooses the best mode automatically)
  3. Set the temperature
    Use the 温度 ▲ / ▼ buttons to adjust the temperature.
    Recommended: 24–27 °C in summer, 20–23 °C in winter.
  4. Adjust fan speed
    Press 風量 (Fan speed) to change the airflow strength.
  5. Adjust wind direction
    Use 風向 上下 / 左右 buttons to move the air up/down or left/right.

💡 Please close windows & doors while using the AC for better efficiency.
⚠ Always turn OFF the AC when you leave the room or the house.

Vacuum Cleaner

  1. Turn ON / OFF — Press the round power button at the top (shown in the red circle).
  2. Use — Gently push the vacuum over the floor or carpet. Please avoid vacuuming water or very wet areas.
  3. Empty the dust — After cleaning, make sure the vacuum is OFF, then press the lever in the blue box to open the dust container and empty it into the trash.
  4. Close & return — Close the container firmly and place the vacuum back in its original spot.

If the suction feels weak, please check and empty the dust container.

Microwave Oven (SHARP)

  1. Open the door and place your food or drink inside on the plate.
  2. Choose the mode:
    • 500W — Standard heating (recommended)
    • Defrost — Use for frozen food
    Press the mode button (highlighted in the picture) to switch modes.
  3. Rotate the timer dial to set the heating time.
    *E.g. 1–2 min for warming drinks, 3–5 min for meals.
  4. The microwave will start automatically** after turning the dial.
  5. When the time is up, it will stop and make a sound. Carefully remove the food.

⚠ Please do not use metal containers, aluminum foil, or sealed containers inside the microwave. Use microwave-safe dishes only.

Rice Cooker (TIger IH)

  1. Open the lid and take out the inner pot.
  2. Put rice and water inside the pot.
    Example: 1 cup of rice (180 ml) + water up to level “1”.
    *This is for non-washed rice (無洗米).
  3. Wipe the outside of the pot if it gets wet and place it back in the cooker.
  4. Close the lid completely.
  5. Press the “炊飯” (Start) button to begin cooking.
  6. Cooking takes around 40–50 minutes. The cooker will automatically switch to Warm mode (保温) when done.

After cooking:

  • Use the 保温 (Warm) button to keep rice warm if you plan to eat later.
  • Press 取消 (Cancel) to stop any process.

⚠ Please do not put the inner pot directly on the stove or in the sink with water inside.
Always clean and dry it before returning.

Floor Heating

The house has floor heating and system. Please follow the instructions below.

① Floor Heating Controller (Living Room)

  1. Press “運転/停止1” or “運転/停止2” to turn ON floor heating.
  2. To adjust temperature:
    • Press & hold the “設定” button (blue circle) to enter setting mode.
    • Use ▲ / ▼ buttons to adjust the temperature.
    • Press & hold “設定” button again to save.

Heating may take some time to warm up. Please turn OFF when not needed.

Gas Stove & Small Oven Instructions

Please follow these steps carefully when using the stove.
⚠ Always turn off the flame after use. Never leave the stove unattended.

  1. Place your pot or pan securely on the burner.
  2. Press the control knob down and hold for about 3 seconds to ignite.
    • Left burner → left knob
    • Right burner → right knob
  3. Adjust flame strength by rotating the knob.
  4. To turn off the heat, press the knob down again until the flame goes out.

🔥 *If ignition does not work, release and try again.* Please ensure there is no water or oil around the burner.

Small Oven / Grill

  1. Pull the oven tray forward gently.
  2. Place food inside (please use foil if needed).
  3. Use the oven control knob (center panel) to heat.
  4. Press and hold the knob (same as stove) for ignition.
  5. Keep watching while cooking — it heats quickly.
  6. Press the knob again to turn off.

⚠ Use with caution — this small oven heats very fast and may burn food quickly.
Recommended time: 3–5 min with constant monitoring.

💡 After cooking:
– Turn off all flames
– Ensure no gas smell remains
– Clean any spills after the stove cools down

Dishwasher

  1. Open the dishwasher and place the dishes inside.
    *Please remove large food residues before loading.*
  2. Press & hold the power button (スタート / 電源切) to turn on.
    *It requires a long press to activate.*
  3. Select a mode using the “コース” (Mode) button:
    • 標準 — Standard cleaning (recommended)
    • 念入り — Intensive cleaning
    • 予約 — Timer / Delay
    • 乾燥 — Drying only
  4. Press the Start button to begin the washing cycle.
  5. After washing, the dishwasher will stop automatically.
    You may open the door slightly to release steam and help drying.

🧽 Detergent: If detergent is needed, please add it to the dispenser tray. Many dishes clean well with the standard cycle.

⚠ Please do not wash wooden utensils, kitchen knives, or delicate items.
💡 For best results, avoid overloading and place bowls facing downward.

Water Heater Panel

⚠️ Do NOT turn OFF the water heater switch — it must remain ON to supply hot water and heating.
  1. Power Switch (運転)
    This button turns the water heater ON / OFF.
    ⚠ Please do not turn OFF the water heater during your stay. If it is off, there will be no hot water.
  2. Temperature Adjustment (給湯 温度調節)
    Use the ▲ / ▼ buttons to change the hot-water temperature.
    Recommended setting: 40–43 °C for showers and baths.

💡 If the water feels too hot or too cold, please adjust here rather than at the shower tap.

② Water Heater (Hot Water for Shower / Bath)

⚠️ Please DO NOT turn OFF the main power switch (運転).

  1. To use hot water, ensure the green light next to “運転 (Power)” is ON.
  2. Adjust the temperature by pressing:
    • ▲ Up to increase temperature
    • ▼ Down to decrease
  3. Recommended temperature: 40–43 °C.

If the green light is OFF, please press “運転” to turn it on.

💡 Tips:
– Always check the temperature before showering.
– If no hot water comes out, check that the green light is ON.
– Please turn off floor heating after use to save energy.

Shower / Bathroom Faucet

Lighting Remote Controls

Some rooms have different lighting remotes, but the basic usage is the same: Power ON/OFF, Full brightness, Brighten, Darken.

Room 1 – Lighting Remote

  • Power – Press the green power button to turn the light ON/OFF.
  • Full light (全灯) – Press the center 全灯 button for full brightness.
  • Brighten (明) – Press the upper button to make the room brighter.
  • Darken (暗) – Press the lower button to dim the light.

Room 2 – Lighting Remote (TOSHIBA)

  • Power ON (点灯) – Press the center 点灯 button.
  • Full brightness (全灯) – Press the top 全灯 button.
  • Brighten (明) – Press the upper part of the ring (明).
  • Darken (暗) – Press the lower part of the ring (暗).
  • Power OFF (消灯) – Press the 消灯 button to turn the light off.

Room 3 – Lighting Remote (IRIS OHYAMA)

  • Power ON / Full brightness (点灯・全灯) – Press the large button labeled 点灯 / 全灯.
  • Brighten (明るく) – Press the 明るく button.
  • Darken (暗く) – Press the 暗く button.
  • Power OFF (消灯) – Press the 消灯 button to switch the light off.

💡 Tip: If a light does not respond, please point the remote directly at the ceiling light and try again.

Burnable Garbage
  • Kitchen waste
  • Paper scraps
  • Clothing, etc.
Recyclable Garbage
  • Plastic bottles
  • Container jars & cans
  • Aerosol spray cans, etc.

Garbage disposal II

Except for the rules above, please do not leave used luggage or any big items in the house. If you leave items that are not permitted, we may ask you to pay for the costs incurred.

Quiet time: 9pm–8am

Please respect our residential community. Between 9pm and 8am, keep noise to a minimum.

Please take off your shoes

Please do not wear your shoes inside the property.

Smoking rules

This is a non‑smoking home — no smoking or vaping inside the house.

Parking is not available on-site.

If you are arriving by car, please use one of the nearby coin-operated parking lots.

security
